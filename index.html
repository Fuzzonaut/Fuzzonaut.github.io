Fuzzonaut

Fuzzonaut is the name I go by online. He’s the part of me that can get just as absorbed in an abstract math question as in figuring out how to make something work. That curiosity is what ties together the math, machine learning, games, and side projects on my website.

The name carries some of my taste in music, too. Think guitar fuzz, stoner rock, and the spacey side of psychedelia. If you need a feel for his personality, imagine someone who takes the question “I wonder how that works” seriously enough to disappear into it for a weekend.
